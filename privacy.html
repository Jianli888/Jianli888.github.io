GrainGrid

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

GrainGrid is designed as an offline-first single-player puzzle game.

Data We Collect

GrainGrid does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal data in this version.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • in-progress game session
  • best score
  • cumulative gameplay stats
  • onboarding completion state
  • local settings such as sound and haptics preferences

This data is used only to preserve your experience in the app.

Analytics And Advertising

This version of GrainGrid does not include:

  • third-party analytics SDKs
  • advertising SDKs
  • account systems
  • social sharing integrations

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
