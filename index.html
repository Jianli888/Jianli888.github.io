Jian’s story

Born in Chongqing.
Made in Winterthur.

When Jian Li moved from Chongqing to Switzerland, one thing was missing: chili sauce with real depth.

So she turned to her family recipe—layering heat with garlic, ginger, red onion and fragrant Sichuan pepper oil. What began as jars shared with classmates at the University of Zurich grew into a small-batch sauce made for fondue, noodles, grilled meats and everything in between.

— Jian
